### Hi, I'm Anzuruku Jacob 👋

Full-stack developer based in East Africa, building digital tools for problems I've watched play out in real communities — not hypothetical ones.

I direct AI tools to write code, and I'm studying Computer Science (University of the People) to get sharper at judging, catching, and fixing what they generate. Not stepping away from AI — leveling up how I use it.

---

**🌍 What I'm building**

- **[Sudd Library](https://sudd-library.anznexus.workers.dev/)** — offline-first, open-access curriculum library for South Sudanese schools. Born out of volunteering as a teacher in Nimule and watching schools scrape by on Ugandan and Kenyan materials for lack of national ones. Built as a PWA, deployed on Cloudflare Workers, works without internet.
- **SokoNort** — local web marketplace live for Adjumani, Uganda. Registration, image uploads, and security are battle-tested; the rest is shipping.

**🛠️ Stack**

`JavaScript` `HTML5/CSS3` `Tailwind CSS` `PWA`
`Cloudflare Workers/Pages/R2` `Firebase` `Supabase` `Vercel`
AI orchestration: Google AI Studio, Claude Code, n8n, MCP

**📚 Currently**

Deep in a Computer Science degree (University of the People) and a self-built backend/cloud specialization track — building the foundation to match the ambition.
